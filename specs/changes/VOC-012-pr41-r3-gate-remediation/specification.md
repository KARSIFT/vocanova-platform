release, issue closure, or autonomous authority.

  evidence before PR #40 remediation may resume.
- **VOC-012-R11:** Keep issue #39 open and keep VOC-010/VOC-006 correction and F2-I04

- **VOC-012-R12:** Change no application, dependency, workflow, governance-authority,

## Decisions and effects

- **VOC-012-D01:** A chat-only report does not satisfy a canonical pre-merge evidence
  requirement.
- **VOC-012-D02:** Remediation is governed revert plus fresh adoption, not retroactive
  evidence posting.
- **VOC-012-D03:** Routine R3 needs no standing founder/steward approval merely because
  it is R3, but independent verification and authorized human merge remain mandatory.
- **VOC-012-D04:** VOC-011 cannot authorize PR #40 remediation until its own valid
  fresh adoption and final synchronization are complete.

The package has no runtime, data, security, privacy, analytics, accessibility,
deployment, production, or release effect.
